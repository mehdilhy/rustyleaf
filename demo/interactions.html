🍂 Rustyleaf — Interactions

Try it: drag (momentum) · scroll to zoom · Shift+drag for box zoom · click the map, then ←↑↓→ pan and +/− zoom · on touch screens: one-finger pan, two-finger pinch.

The GeoJSON layer uses onEachFeature — click a monument dot for a popup, hover for a tooltip. Every map event streams into the log (bottom right).
