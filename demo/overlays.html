🍂 Rustyleaf — Overlays & Tiles

ImageOverlay and SVGOverlay are pinned to LatLng bounds and repositioned every frame. The debug GridLayer draws a DOM tile per z/x/y, and the WMSTileLayer requests GetMap tiles with a per-tile EPSG:3857 bbox computed in the Rust core.

Demos: points · markers · shapes · interactions
